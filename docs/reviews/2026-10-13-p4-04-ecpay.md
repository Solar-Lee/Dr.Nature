# P4-04 綠界金流第二輪審查紀錄（2026-10-13）

範圍：P4-03＋P4-04 全部金流程式。4 視角、每條 2 名懷疑者，共 30 個代理。

| 票數 | 嚴重度 | ID | 摘要 | 處置 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2/2 | medium | F1 | When a ReturnURL notice arrives for a canceled order, the route always treats it as 'paid after cancel'. It never checks whether this session's payment was already captured before the cancel and already refunded. A duplicate notice after a normal paid-then-canceled order therefore raises a false [ECPAY-ALERT] asking staff to refund again. | 修正：已取消訂單的通知先查此 session 是否已有入帳付款；有則視為重複通知（info、ACK），不再告警或要求重複退款。新增測試＋反向測試。 |
| 2/2 | low | ecpay-client-injected-ecpay-keys | providerKeys() is meant to drop client-supplied session data, but it copies the ecpay_payment_instructions, ecpay_payment_info, ecpay_notice and ecpay_trade_info keys through without checking them. Medusa merges the storefront's data into payment_session.data when the session is created, so a shopper can plant those keys. authorizePayment then carries them into the session and the payment record. | 修正：initiate／updatePayment 明確把 ecpay_* 鍵設為 null 覆蓋顧客植入值；providerKeys 只保留驗證通過的證明，付款指示一律由已驗章的取號通知重建。新增單元＋整合測試＋反向測試。 |
| 2/2 | low | ecpay-p404-test-unsupported-method-false-green | The 'rejects unsupported method' case still uses choosePayment 'ATM', which P4-04 made a supported method.
